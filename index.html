How to Play

Everything you need before your first move.

🎯 Goal of the game

Get three of your symbols in a row — across, down, or diagonally — before anyone else does. If all 9 boxes fill up with no winner, the game is a draw.

🕹️ How a game works

  1. Pick a mode on the Home screen: 1 Player vs Computer, or Multiplayer.
  2. Press Start Game.
  3. Players take turns tapping an empty box.
  4. The winning line lights up, and the winner's score goes up by 1.
  5. Press Restart Game for a new round — scores stay. Press Reset Scores to start scores from zero.

🎨 Player symbols and colours

Every player has a different symbol and colour, so turns are easy to follow on the scoreboard.

🤖 Playing against the computer

You play X and the computer plays O. The computer is not random: first it tries to win, then it blocks your win, then it takes the centre, then a corner.

Undo in this mode takes back both your move and the computer's move, so it is your turn again. In multiplayer, Undo takes back just one move.

💡 Tips

  • Take the centre first if it is free — it is part of 4 winning lines.
  • Watch for two of your symbols in a line, and two of your opponent's.
  • With 5 or 6 players the board still has only 9 boxes, so each player gets just 1–2 turns and draws are much more common. That is a 3×3 board limit, not a bug.
